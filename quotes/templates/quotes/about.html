About

Born in 1967, David Berman was the lead singer of the band Silver Jews. He was the only constant member of the band, making music under the name from 1989 to 2009. Berman was known for his abstract lyricism and unorthodox approach to songwriting. He released a final solo project in 2019 called Purple Mountains and passed away the next month.
